## 5. Verdict: **FAIL**
All 10 categories are below 8 (scores 4-6), and seven auto-fail items are present (inconsistency, unnatural animation, composition, lighting jump, rendering artefacts, flatness, elements outside the art direction).

**Top 3 fixes, ranked by visual gain per hour of work**
1. **Remove the editing and render glitches (about 1-2 h, biggest gain per hour).** f164 (keep Chad in B03b's first frame or start the whip at f160), f706 (drop the stray wrong-pose frame at the whip-in), f1527 (one continuous B21/B22 shot with one light state, or a real cut to a new angle), f2191-2206 (an ink-edged puff `DustWipe` that never blanks the whole frame for more than ~4 f, and show the cart hitting the counter before it), and the B25/B27 stair-stepped orange band + SALE halo (raise or filter the occlusion/light-map resolution in `kit/toon`, or swap the band for a painted beam card). Files: `story.ts`, `kit/toon` paint/occlusion. Clears 4 of the auto-fail items.
2. **Animate the verbs the narration and SFX already mark (about 3-4 h).** Knock with contact on a visible door (B06, f462), tape strip pulled on "tape" (B15, f1033/f1061), battery slid in + click (B21/B22, f1483/f1556), kick that connects (B26, f1759), generator jolt + needle drop + an ink smoke ring in frame (B23, f1622-1644), toys open mouths with ink sound rings on "scream" (B28, f1874), and the crash (B30b, f2175). Add a 3-step `exprLadder()` on B06, B21, B31, B32. Files: `story.ts`, `props.ts`, `roles.ts`. Lifts Animation, Characters, Composition and polish together.
3. **Bring the x-ray and effects into the house style, and make practicals act on the scene (about 3-5 h).** Replace the neon HUD with an in-world painted cutaway the camera dives into (the duck from B01 opened like ref3's muzzle cutaway, ink-lined parts, the scene's light), redraw megaphone rings, rainbow trail and cart glow as ink-edged painted shapes, and have the lantern tint your face (B09/B10), the dying floodlight darken the roof (B24) and a warm interior light back the shutter reveal (B25). Fixes the art-direction auto-fail and lifts colourfulness/luma toward the bar. Files: `story.ts` (insert), `kit/toon` effects, `build.ts` lights.

**Real technical limits and the practical alternative**
- A per-agent crowd run cycle for ~400 cards may be heavy at `--concurrency=1` on a 4 GB GPU. Alternative: animate the 20-30 nearest zombies fully (stride, lean, stagger offsets) and keep the far ones as instanced cards with randomized bob phases and a ragged formation, never a grid.
- Continuous moves > 6 s compete with the 2-3 s shot rhythm of a 248-word script. Alternative: make B27 + B28 one ride (cart POV -> crane up over the street, ~7 s) instead of a cut.

What I did not check: audio by ear (sound judged from loudness.json/metrics only); my per-shot motion numbers use my own scale, not ref_compare.py's.
