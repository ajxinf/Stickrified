Researched 2026-11-16. Every stated fact in the narration, with its source. Lines not listed are framing or jokes.

## Discrepancies
- Start date: officially 4 April 1995; Dailly's own prototype CD is dated November 1994 and design meetings ran from January 1995. The video says "a year earlier" for the engine (1994) and puts the bug in 1995 ("DUNDEE, 1995"), which fits both.
- The police bug: popular, consistent across retellings, but unsourced from the team itself. Treated as legend on screen and in narration.
- The publicity campaign behind the press outrage was run by Max Clifford (hired via the publisher), later convicted of sex offences. He is deliberately not named or shown.

## Comic exaggeration (no viewer should take these as fact)
- The rugby-scrum pile-up, the BUG stamp, the yawning cop and robber, cars handling "like shopping carts", the giant CANCEL stamp growing every week, the coffee-cup tower, the lords shaking fists in unison, the queue round the block.
- "You" are an unnamed viewpoint character in the office, not a real person; Mike and Dave's on-screen actions are dramatised, their roles are real.
