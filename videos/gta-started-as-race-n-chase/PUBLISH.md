## Description
Before GTA was GTA, it was Race'n'Chase: a top-down game from Dundee where you played the cop. It was boring, it
crashed all the time, and the publisher kept trying to cancel it. Then, as the legend goes, the police started
ramming you.

Sources: DMA Design history (Wikipedia, "Grand Theft Auto (video game)"); Mike Dailly on lemmings.info; GameSpot and
Game Informer interviews with Gary Penn (2011); Engadget, "Race'n'Chase: Original GTA design docs" (2011); Take-Two
results. The police-ramming bug is a widely told legend and is presented as one. Full list in sources.md.
