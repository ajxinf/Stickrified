## Facts

- Fiction: no sources needed. One rule, said in line 1-2 and shown before it matters: every promise grows a flower out of the promiser's skin; kept = it blooms, broken = it rots and stinks.
- Fallout of the same rule (not new rules): a promise that is already false rots the moment it is said (Derek's nose flower); bees land only on real flowers (planted in line 9, shown with a bee snubbing Derek).
- Derek's 1,000 blooms are glued-on fakes hiding 1,000 rotten real ones; the clue is that his flowers bloom the second he promises (a real one only blooms once the promise is kept).
- Numbers: 1,000 (Derek's flowers, the motif), 10,000 bees, 99 % in the polls: story numbers.
