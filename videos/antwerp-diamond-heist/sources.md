## Discrepancies
- **Box count**: 109 of 189 [W] vs 123 of 160 (early reports). We use [W].
- **Value**: $100 million+ is the standard figure; Notarbartolo told Wired it was about $20 million and an insurance fraud. Experts and police reject his account [W], [CJR]. The vault had in fact been denied insurance [W].
- **Ten layers**: Wired's count; one retelling says eight. The video only shows the layers it names (heat sensor, radar, magnets, light sensor, lock).
- **Notarbartolo's Wired story** (a hidden camera above the vault door, a replica vault for rehearsal, the key left in a storage room) is disputed by lead detective Agim De Bruycker: "Not one detail of Leo's story can be confirmed" [CJR]. None of it is narrated as fact; line 18 says nobody knows how the lock was opened.
- **Date**: most sources say 15-16 Feb; some say 14-16 or 17 (the discovery was Monday 17 Feb).

## Comic exaggeration (no viewer should take these as fact)
- "One for every dollar" (a ratio joke), numbers pouring out of the dial, the onion of glowing shields.
- "The only thing that can stop you is a sandwich" (line 2: a tease of the real ending, the DNA sandwich).
- "Day one / day ten / day five hundred" with the guard: dramatised (sources only say staff got used to him), the most serious face in Belgium, "like every game, you grind setup missions".
- The espresso-cup tower, the giant loupe eye, the guard waving without looking up, the pigeon, the slow-motion walk.
- "Like a kid hiding behind a pizza box", "ankle-deep in diamonds" (the vault floor was strewn with gems and papers, not ankle-deep), trash "like confetti", steam from the landowner's ears, the glowing sandwich, Speedy eating another sandwich.
- Speedy panicking at pigeons; Speedy's two lines ("We're gonna get caught", "Just drive!") are invented and kept vague.
- Hairspray "like breath on cold glasses" is a simplification of how the coating blinded the sensor.
