## Facts

- True history: every stated fact is in sources.md. Main sources: Wikipedia 'Antwerp diamond heist' (which draws on the book Flawless, the police-side account) and CJR 2010 on the Wired 2009 story.
- 'You' = the inside man (Leonardo Notarbartolo, the convicted leader, a public figure in true facts only; never named in the narration, no invented thoughts beyond 'you visit until the guards stop looking').
- Notarbartolo's own Wired account (hidden camera in the vault, insurance fraud, only $20 million) is disputed by the police and is NOT used as fact. How the combination lock was beaten is unknown, and the narration says so.
- Speedy's two lines are invented, short and vague (Speedy is described in every account as anxious and paranoid; he scattered the trash).
- No game logos, game art or game characters: the 'heist mission' feel is our own (mission cards live on props: a notebook, a crew board).
- Numbers: ten layers (the motif: hook, finale count, loop); a hundred million (combinations and dollars, the joke); 109 of 189 boxes; five days to the arrest.
