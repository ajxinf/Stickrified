## Description
In 2003, five men walked into the safest vault on Earth, two floors under Antwerp's diamond district, and walked out with over $100 million. Ten layers of security, beaten with hairspray, foam and tape. Then they made one mistake: the trash.

The true story of the Antwerp Diamond Centre heist (15-16 February 2003). Most of the diamonds were never found. How the 100-million-combination lock was opened is still a mystery.

Sources: Wikipedia, "Antwerp diamond heist" (and the book Flawless by Selby & Campbell); Columbia Journalism Review (2010) on the Wired story; Guinness World Records, "Largest diamond heist". Some scenes are dramatised for comedy.
