# visual-ref1/2/3 notes (every 4th frame; same family as ours: painted 3D world + round-head 2D cards)
## ref1 00 (0-4.7 s) lottery clerk
- Dense, specific set: lottery-ticket wall (dozens of coloured tickets), pendant lamp over the head (motivated key), "LIVE BAIT" painted counter front, register screen "READY"/"WINNER! $1,000,000" (text in world drives the beat).
- ONE continuous camera move instead of cuts: medium (slight push) -> dives over the shoulder to the ticket under the scanner (1.2-1.6) -> macro with the scanner as a blurred FOREGROUND block and a red laser line -> whips back up to the face (2.5-2.7). Motion blur on the dives.
- Reaction is a sequence, not a swap: neutral -> ring eyes + O mouth + sweat drop (2.7) -> eyes slide sideways, sly (3.6) -> he flips the register display away (prop action, 3.7) -> lip-synced "Sorry, man" with a hand slide of the ticket.
- Heads take light: a soft grey shade on the far side and under the chin; cream, not white. Hand-drawn hair with ink strands.
- Framing: chest-up mediums, camera slightly low; background soft but readable.
## ref1 01 (4.8-9.5 s)
- Hands sell the lie: he holds the ticket up, tucks it behind his back, raises a finger on "Not a winner" (pose change every ~0.3 s, all on words). Lip sync per syllable, big mouth shapes.
- 6.0 s: DISSOLVE (8 f) into an OVER-THE-SHOULDER reverse: the clerk's head is a huge blurred dark foreground mass at frame left (~35 % of frame), the customer framed small in the gap, register in fg. Classic shot/reverse-shot with a dirty foreground = instant depth.
- Customer has a signature costume (bucket hat with fishing lures, yellow overalls) readable at phone size; frowny resignation acting with lip sync.
- "walks out": he turns his BACK to camera (back view of the card: hair from behind) and walks away into depth toward the window, shrinking; we see a door/window in the background. Characters have back views and walk into depth.
## ref1 02 (9.6-14.3 s)
- Door closes in the deep background while the fg shoulder holds: action happens in DEPTH, the camera waits.
- 10.6 dissolve to a gag shot: the clerk is upside down head-first in a trash can, only legs waving, trash (cups, banana peel) FLYING out in arcs, tumbling (physics, secondary motion); camera pushes in.
- 12.4: a SOFT CIRCULAR VIGNETTE "iris" POV (looking out of the trash can): his face framed in a bright disc against a dark rim (the can's inside), bright glow edge = in-world framing device. He grins, googly eyes, slides the ticket out.
- Transitions are 6-10 f dissolves, often with a matched shape (round can -> round iris).
## ref1 03 (14.4-19.1 s)

Then a continuous TILT UP from the wallet to his blissful closed-eye smile (16.0-16.4): one move links insert and face.
- 17.1: dissolve (shape match: round face -> giant moon) into a night scene: huge pink moon BACKLIGHTS the character (silhouette + rim), magenta-to-indigo sky, castle silhouette, lily pads, water. Boat named "FINDERS KEEPERS" (pun in the set). Camera ARCS around the boat (17.6-19.1), string lights come on. Colour script: warm interior -> cool magenta night = a strong mood contrast between scenes.
## ref1 04 (19.2-23.9 s)
- The fantasy builds IN-SHOT: characters pop into the hot tub one by one on each listed noun ("whole new set of friends"), fireworks bloom behind the castle; the boat sails away into depth (scale shrink) while the camera holds.
- 21.7 THE FANTASY REVEALED AS AN OBJECT: the whole moonlit scene shrinks into a FISHBOWL on his nightstand (camera pulls out through the glass): a match-cut transition that is also the joke ("all in his head"). Then: he sleeps on a bare mattress, pizza box, cool-blue room lit by ONE bare bulb (warm pool, cool fill).
- 22.9-23.6 "the next morning": a SUNRISE FLARE from the window floods the frame (light as transition), dissolve to a road at golden hour with the red car driving at the lens.
- Lighting tells time: cool night with one warm bulb -> warm sunrise flare -> golden road.
## ref1 05 (24.0-28.7 s)

## ref1 06 (28.8-33.5 s)
- A 4 s dialogue hold on one character, kept alive by: lip sync, big mouth shapes, the ticket raised and shown, a point, and then she PRESSES A RED BUTTON under the counter that LIGHTS UP red (a practical that reacts; the hidden alarm = the gag planted visually while she smiles).
- Glass reflections drift across her (the pane is between us): reflections are a cheap, constant layer of motion.
- 32.6 dissolve to a waiting room: "OUR WINNERS" wall with framed winner photos, fish tank (moving water/bubbles), plastic chairs, a star emblem in the floor; hero in a SUIT (costume changed for the occasion), sitting still, blinking, deadpan. Wide, symmetrical, frontal composition used deliberately for a "waiting" beat (stillness as a joke).
## ref1 07 (33.6-38.3 s)
- Time passing shown by POSE CHANGES in one continuous shot, not cuts: slumped and bored head back (5 minutes) -> slouched further (go by) -> bounces up, both arms raised, grin ("Then ten until finally"). The camera slowly arcs and pushes the whole time (~5 s), never still.
- Fish in the tank swims; the pufferfish turns its face to camera (environmental life + a hidden gag).
- 36.4: the door in the background OPENS and a figure appears as a SILHOUETTE BACKLIT by a bright room behind it (strong value contrast in a busy yellow frame = instant read), walks forward and is revealed as the customer from the start (bucket hat): the reveal is staged in depth, behind the hero, in focus.
## ref1 08 (38.4-43.1 s)
- The background character keeps acting while the hero stays frozen in his pose (arms still up, grin locked): the customer walks forward, REMOVES his bucket hat (a costume reveal: he is now in a suit with a badge on his belt), two police officers walk in behind him one after another on "followed by two police officers". The listing in the narration is staged as entrances in depth.
- The hero's grin decays in steps on each beat: grin -> one eye squints -> ring eyes -> flat line -> worried mouth (an expression ladder, ~0.3 s per step).
- Composition: hero big in the fg-right, the threat building up behind in the mid-ground left; hierarchy by size and focus.
## ref1 09 (43.2-47.9 s) the explainer turn
- 43.6: the frame FREEZES and becomes a POLAROID PHOTO ("KYLE · LAKE RD" handwritten) that the camera pulls back from: it is pinned to an investigator's cork board, a hand (stroke arm) pins it. A freeze-frame-to-object transition.
- The board: dozens of polaroids of other clerks in a tall column labelled "CLERKS" vs a single photo under "EVERYONE ELSE" = a DATA VISUALISATION built from physical props in the world (a bar chart made of photos). The camera cranes up the column to show its height, then pans to the lonely single photo.
- VENETIAN-BLIND LIGHT: diagonal stripes of warm light and cool shadow fall across the board and the blue brick wall (film-noir office cue), consistent across the whole sequence.
## ref1 10 (48.0-52.7 s)

## ref1 11 (52.8-57.5 s) loop back

## ref2 00 (0-4.7 s) scratchcard hook, warm office
- Low-key warm office: green desk lamp as the key (left), deep brown shadows, a red alarm light on the back cabinet. Dark background, bright warm desk = the subject pops by VALUE, not by colour.
- Hook: he holds up two scratchcards to the lens; the camera DROPS to desk level as he lays them down (1.3-1.6), revealing the cards in the foreground.
- PROPS EMIT LIGHT ON THE CHARACTER: the winning card glows gold, sparkles rise, and the glow re-lights his face and body gold (2.1); the losing card emits a sickly GREEN stink haze with a fly and his face turns green (3.3-4.0). Light colour = the emotion of the object; the character literally takes the light of what he looks at.
- Ink outlines take the light colour too (golden lit, greenish). Expression: ring eyes, wink, gritted teeth, smug.
